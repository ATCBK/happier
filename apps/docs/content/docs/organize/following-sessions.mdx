---
title: Following a session
description: Decide which sessions push updates at you, at what volume, without changing who can access them.
---

Access and attention are different questions. Somebody sharing a session with a
Team of forty answers the first one; **Follow** answers the second. Follow a
session and its activity reaches you — notifications, Voice context, the personal
surfaces you already use. Leave it unfollowed and it sits quietly in the list
until you go looking.

The rule underneath is worth stating once: **Following never changes who can
access this session.** It is your subscription, not a grant.

> **Availability:** Follow is in the current unreleased 0.3 checkout. Stable 0.2 has no Follow subscription — check the version of the server binary you are running.

## Availability

Follow is a per-Home capability. A self-hosted Home offers it unless its operator
sets `HAPPIER_FEATURE_SESSIONS_FOLLOWING__ENABLED` to a false value.

## Follow one session

Open the session and choose **Follow**. The editor — **Follow this session** —
carries one choice, how much you want to hear:

| Level | What reaches you |
| --- | --- |
| **No notifications** | The session is followed, but it does not notify you. |
| **Important updates** | The activity Happier classifies as worth interrupting you for. |
| **Every new message** | Each new message in the session. |

**Include in Voice** is separate and optional: it lets Voice keep the session in
context. An encrypted session has to be unlocked on this device before Voice can
include it, and some Voice modes cannot include stored session updates at all —
Happier says which case you are in rather than silently dropping the session.

Owning a session supplies implicit tracking. Your notification policy and explicit
opt-out still apply; ownership does not force every update to interrupt you.

## Sessions you follow without asking

Some relationships imply attention, so Happier can follow on your behalf. In
**Settings → Notifications**, **Automatically follow** has four switches:

| Switch | Default |
| --- | --- |
| **Sessions assigned to me** | On |
| **Sessions shared directly** | Off |
| **Sessions shared through Teams** | Off |
| **Sessions shared through Groups** | Off |

The defaults are deliberate. Being handed a session personally is a request for
your attention; being inside the audience of a Team share usually is not — a Team
of forty would otherwise generate forty subscriptions every time anybody shared
anything. Turn the Team and Group switches on if your Team is small enough that
you do want all of it.

In current 0.3 development, these preferences are default Follow rules for future
matching activity. Assignment and sharing do not create an individual Follow
subscription or read-state baseline. Your explicit Follow choices, opt-outs and
existing read state stay unchanged when defaults change. These preferences are
set per Home.

## Follow, read state, and assignment

Three related things, each answering something different:

- **Follow** decides whether future activity reaches you.
- **Read and unread state** decides whether the session currently wants a look.
  See [Read and unread state](/organize/session-read-state).
- **Assignment** — the responsible member on a shared session — records who is
  expected to act. Your default Follow rule can select its later activity without
  creating an individual subscription.

Receiving Team or Group access on its own creates none of these. It does not
produce unread state, Inbox pressure, a badge, or a notification. Open the session
and follow it when you want it in your flow.

## Notify me once

In the unreleased 0.3 checkout, **Notify me** uses your private Follow choice.
Choose the current turn finishing, including a failed or stopped outcome, or
the first new request that needs you. The current-turn option stays bound to
that turn; it does not carry over to the next one.

An accepted Inbox item uses up the one-shot even if push delivery is unavailable.
Turning the option off clears only the one-shot. Your ongoing Follow choice,
Include in Voice and read state stay separate.

## Mute a source (0.3 development)

Mute is separate from Unfollow: it stops future delivery from one exact sender
session or Follow source to one receiver, without removing that receiver's Follow
or access. Removing the mute lets future delivery resume; it does not undo work
already accepted. The current unreleased 0.3 source has the private
`session.delivery.mute.set`, `.remove` and `.list` Action/API contract. The
user-facing Mute control and delivery integrations are still in development;
this is not a claim that every route is available in a shipped app.

Older notification shortcuts may still appear as ordinary Habits. Happier keeps
them because they cannot reliably be distinguished from Habits you authored.
Review or delete them individually if you no longer need them; authored
notification workflows continue to work.

## When following pauses or stops

- **Following is paused while this session is archived.** Unarchive it to resume.
- **You no longer have access to this session.** Follow ends with the access it
  depended on.
- **You're offline. Reconnect to change following.** Your existing follows keep
  working; only changing them needs the Home.

## Follow between sessions

An agent session can also follow *another session* and receive bounded context
from it during a turn, using the same Follow event and delivery policy owners.
That is **Session updates** in the same editor — **Follow in another Session…** —
and it is about what the agent reads, not about what you are notified of. It is
described under [Session-to-Session Follow
context](/accounts/session-sharing#session-to-session-follow-context-03-development).
